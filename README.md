Entry for the make.fun bounty [news-image-20261126](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126): sum up the world's news of Thursday 26 November 2026 in one image.

The image is a newspaper front page — masthead dated 26 November 2026, a central globe ringed by six numbered vignettes, and a legend naming the day's top stories:

1. Pakistan's first astronaut reaches China's space station
2. Oil tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz
3. Climate talks end without a deal
4. Ukraine strikes Russian oil refineries
5. US prepares new global tariffs
6. Cricket record: 204 runs off 53 balls, aged 15

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
